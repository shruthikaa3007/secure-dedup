Secure Deduplication Prototype

Reviewer-ready secure deduplication demo.

This demo is designed for presentation: it creates a fresh session, stores encrypted chunks by default, walks through duplicate verification step by step, and keeps behavioural monitoring visible without turning the screen into an operator console.

Reviewer Story

Encrypt once, challenge duplicates, monitor behaviour.

Base paper: Wu et al., JISA 2024 (randomized dedup encryption against frequency attack).

Improvement: a lighter secret-assisted fingerprint-bound AES-GCM design, plus visible PoW retry and lightweight behavioural monitoring.
